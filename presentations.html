Graduate Seminar Presentations

Introduction to Survival Analysis

Pramesh Subedi, Ph.D.
Department of Mathematics, Ohio University
Graduate Seminar, October 2026

This presentation introduces fundamental concepts and methods in survival analysis, including censoring, survival and hazard functions, Kaplan-Meier estimation, the Nelson-Aalen estimator, log-rank tests, Cox proportional hazards models, and additive hazards models, with applications using R.
